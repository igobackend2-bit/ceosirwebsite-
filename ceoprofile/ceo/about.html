He pioneers precision agriculture and polyhouse cultivation, turning unused land into profitable farms. Dr. John Yesudhas, visionary CEO and founder of IGO Agritech Farms, pioneers tech-driven and sustainable farming innovations. With an engineering background, he revolutionized agriculture through precision farming, polyhouse cultivation, and hydroponics to boost yield and efficiency. Leading a team of agri-engineers and agronomists, he transforms unused land into profitable ventures. As a mentor and speaker, he inspires future agri-entrepreneurs and drives global impact in modern agriculture.
