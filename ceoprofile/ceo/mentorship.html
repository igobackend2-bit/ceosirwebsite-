Dr. John Yesudhas is deeply committed to mentoring college students and shaping the next generation of agri-entrepreneurs and innovators. Through skill development programs, career placement opportunities in IGO Group, and interactive college workshops, he helps students bridge the gap between education and industry. His guidance empowers young minds to gain confidence, practical exposure, and career-ready skills.

As a TEDx speaker and Tech Farming expert, he inspires thousands of students to think beyond conventional paths and embrace innovation in agriculture and technology. By offering interview preparation, real-world project training, and entrepreneurship mentoring, Dr. Yesudhas continues to motivate and equip the youth to lead with vision, creativity, and sustainable impact.
